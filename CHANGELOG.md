# Changelog

## 0.9.7 - 2026-08-07

- Added PWA install support and offline app-shell caching.
- Restored startup sync and recovery triggers after reconnect or foreground.
- Updated install hints and simplified social-link handling.

## 0.9.6 - 2026-08-07

- Improved recipient-page actions, empty-card guidance, and unreadable-link recovery.
- Improved long contact-field rendering and card text overflow handling.
- Added QR-sharing fallback messaging for cards that are too large for QR codes.
- Completed translations for new UI copy and localized Sync wording.

## 0.9.5 - 2026-08-07

- Added social handle/ID support, including Bluesky and WhatsApp.
- Added a card tagline, shown on the card face and exported to vCard NOTE.
- Refined sharing, vCard handoff, QR sizing, and card typography.

## 0.9.4 - 2026-08-06

- Improved vCard download compatibility on newer iOS releases.
- Updated About and Privacy product references to Card Field.

## 0.9.3 - 2026-07-16

- Added encrypted multi-device sync for cards and photo assets.
- Added Settings data export, including vCard and photo-card ZIP exports.
- Added Malay, Tamil, Thai, Vietnamese, and Indonesian support.
- Improved privacy copy, received-card editing, photo previews, and deletion.

## 0.9.2 - 2026-07-16

Initial public release baseline.

- Added the standalone local-first My Card app.
- Added encrypted share links, card management, vCard export, and photo-card
  capture.
- Added language, privacy, AI recognition, and About settings.
- Added social preview metadata and production deployment configuration.
